My teaching and mentoring experience reflects a broad engagement with aviation education across various domains. Across these roles, I focus on helping learners develop technical understanding, sound judgment, and safety-oriented thinking, with the broader goal of contributing to the advancement of aviation safety.

## Flight Instruction

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/teaching/Flight Instruction.jpg"
       alt="Flight instruction"
       style="width: 450px; max-width: 100%; height: auto;">
</p>

As an FAA-certified flight instructor, I have provided flight and ground instruction at [Aviators Academy](https://aviatorsacademy.us/) across multiple stages of pilot training. My instructional experience includes working with students in various aircraft and avionics configurations, with an emphasis on technical proficiency, procedural discipline, aeronautical knowledge, and safety-oriented training practices.

Beyond direct instruction, I have developed student training resources such as pre-solo exam materials and exam-preparation guides. These materials were designed to help students prepare for key training milestones and promote consistency in ground instruction and checkride preparation.

Instructional areas include:

- Private Pilot Certificate (PPL)
- Instrument Rating (IR)
- Commercial Pilot Certificate (CPL)
- Certified Flight Instructor-Instrument (CFI/CFII)
- Flight Review and Instrument Proficiency Check (IPC)
- Complex Airplane Endorsement

## Aviation Mentoring & Outreach

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/teaching/Outreach.jpg"
       alt="Flight instruction"
       style="width: 650px; max-width: 100%; height: auto;">
</p>

In addition to formal teaching roles, I contribute to aviation education outreach and mentorship for prospective pilots and student pilots. My outreach focuses on helping Korean students understand flight training pathways, FAA certification processes, aviation English preparation, and safety-oriented perspectives related to aviation study and flight training in the United States.

Through this work, I aim to support prospective and current aviation students in making informed decisions, navigating training expectations, and developing a practical understanding of aviation safety culture.
